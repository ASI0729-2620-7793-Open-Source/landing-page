Frequently asked questions

We answer your questions about Nubi

Does Nubi replace medical or psychological care?

No. Nubi is operational support for the moment of a crisis; it does not replace diagnosis or care from health professionals.

Does Nubi work without an internet connection?

Nubi is designed so that the SOS Mode guide and downloaded calming resources are available offline, because many families have limited mobile data away from home.

How many caregivers can support the same user?

A profile can have several linked caregivers. The maximum number depends on the chosen plan.

What happens if I select a pictogram on the AAC board and the caregiver is not available?

Nubi plays the pictogram's audio on the device and saves the message to notify the caregiver as soon as they are available.

Benefits

Benefits for both

Tools that support both the caregiver and the neurodivergent user.

For the caregiver

  • Act faster and with more confidence during a crisis.
  • Provide support adapted to the user's needs.
  • Learn from past episodes to improve future interventions.

For the neurodivergent user

  • Regain calm at their own pace with stimuli they like.
  • Express what they need without relying on speech.
  • Have a predictable environment adapted to their sensitivities.
